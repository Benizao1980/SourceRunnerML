### Manuscript framing

Current central result:

> Human C. jejuni isolates in Peru are overwhelmingly poultry-associated, but known-source benchmark performance improves when global diversity is supplemented with geographically representative local genomes. The improvement is driven mainly by better recognition of locally circulating ruminant lineages and persists after controlling for training-set size.

The manuscript should avoid a simplistic “local beats global” framing because the combined Global+Peru model performs best overall.
